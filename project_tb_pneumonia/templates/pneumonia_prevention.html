Pneumonia Prevention 


          
 Precautions for Pneumonia 

          •	accination: The pneumococcal conjugate vaccine (PCV13) protects against 13 types of bacteria that can cause pneumonia. It's given to babies in a series of three or four doses starting at two months old, and the final dose is given before they're 15 months old. Adults 65 and older can get a one-time injection, and people with risk factors may need a booster shot every 5 to 10 years.

          •	Healthy lifestyle: Eat a healthy diet, get regular physical activity, and avoid smoking.

          •	Manage other conditions: If you have a chronic condition like asthma, diabetes, or heart disease, take good care of it.

          •	Avoid people who are sick: If you're sick, stay away from others as much as possible.

          •	Cover your mouth: When you cough or sneeze, cover your nose and mouth with a tissue or your elbow or sleeve.

          •	Wash your hands: Wash your hands often, especially after coughing and sneezing.

          •	Clean and disinfect: Clean and disinfect surfaces that are touched often.

          •	Stay warm: Keep your home warm to reduce your exposure to cold temperatures.
